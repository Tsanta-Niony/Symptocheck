What exactly is SymptoCheck?


An innovative medical diagnostic platform, integrating an advanced machine learning model to analyze symptoms and provide accurate medical predictions.

In Madagascar, the algorithm focuses on 9 major diseases: asthma, COPD, diabetes, HIV, heart disease, malaria, typhoid, and ulcers.



Why 9 diseases?


These diseases were selected due to their high prevalence and significant impact on public health in Madagascar. Environmental changes, such as climate shifts and urbanization, as well as lifestyle and behavioral modifications, have contributed to the rise in these conditions.
