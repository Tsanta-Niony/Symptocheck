SymptoCheck is designed for:

Accurate Diagnosis

Using machine learning algorithms to analyze patient symptoms and provide precise diagnoses for common diseases prevalent in Madagascar.

Data-Driven Insights

To collect and analyze health data that reflects the local context, allowing for tailored healthcare solutions.

Educational Resource

To serve as an informative platform about symptoms, disease prevention, and the importance of seeking medical attention.

Public Health Support

To enhance healthcare accessibility by focusing on diseases like asthma, COPD, diabetes, HIV, heart disease, malaria, typhoid, and ulcers.

Rapid Assessment

To streamline the diagnostic process, enabling users to receive quick feedback on their symptoms and potential health issues.
